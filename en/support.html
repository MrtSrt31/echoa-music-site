Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import my music files?

Tap the “+” button on the Library tab to import music files or folders from your device. Echoa Music supports MP3, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, OGG, Opus, APE, WavPack, Musepack and more.

Does the app work without an internet connection?

Yes. All music you import is stored on your device and works fully offline.

How do I change the theme or accent color?

Go to Settings → Appearance to change the light/dark theme and accent color.
