Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Echoa Music (“the app”, “we”) handles your information. Echoa Music is designed to work entirely offline and does not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

1. Data We Collect

Echoa Music does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers — in fact, we don't operate any servers at all. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies of any kind.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

Your music library, playlists, settings, and preferences (such as theme and equalizer settings) are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or back it up yourself.

3. Discover Feature & Third-Party Services

The optional Discover tab lets you browse and stream royalty-free or publicly available music from third-party catalogs: Jamendo and the Internet Archive (archive.org). When you use this feature, your device communicates directly with these services according to their own privacy policies.

If you choose to connect a Google account to access your YouTube library, Echoa Music requests read-only access via the official YouTube Data API. This connection is optional, can be revoked at any time from Settings, and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

4. Permissions

Echoa Music requests access to your device's media library / files only to let you import and play your own music. This access is used solely for local playback and is never transmitted anywhere.

5. Children's Privacy

Echoa Music is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
